Briefing

Who we are

The Galactic Federation Corps is a Discord server for Helldivers players. We group up for missions at every difficulty, from first-week recruits learning the stratagem menu to veterans chasing the hardest dives.

Friendly fire is a lesson, not an offense. Someone is always online to run a mission with you, explain a loadout or help you finish a major order.

What we run

Daily drops

Open squads form in voice chat throughout the day. Join one or post your own.

Recruit training

New divers get loadout advice, stratagem practice and a patient squad on low difficulties.

Major orders

We coordinate on the community goal so every mission pushes the front line forward.

Hard dives

Organized runs on top difficulties for players who want a real challenge.

Mission generator

Roll your loadout

Pick your squad size and difficulty, then let Super Earth's finest algorithm assign the mission, the gear and the rules. Lock anything you like and roll the rest again. Send the challenge link to your squad so everyone drops with the same plan.
